WELCOME ONBOARD
Operating since 1999, Southern Right Charters has grown an industry-wide reputation as a leading Hermanus whale watching operator within South Africa and worldwide.
Feel the freedom of unbounded horizons as the Atlantic Ocean beckons. From June to December, Hermanus whale watching comes alive with the arrival of the majestic
Southern Right
and
Humpback
whales.
Join us on educational Hermanus whale watching trips during these peak months and immerse yourself in the thriving marine ecosystem.
All tours are weather/sea permitting, and bookings are advisable.

From June to end November it is peak whale season where we welcome the Southern Right & Humpback whales to the bay and offer our Hermanus Whale Watching Experience.
