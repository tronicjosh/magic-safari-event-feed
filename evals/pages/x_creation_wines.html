Experiences at Creation

Welcome to our little piece of paradise on the Hemel-en-Aarde Ridge. Our fine wine and food alchemy is a rare blend of art, science, our people, and a sense of place. We love to share this with you.

  • WINE TASTING
  • SEASONAL PAIRING
  • A LA CARTE
  • BREAKFAST

From our award-winning Estate and Ridge ranges to the iconic Art of Creation wines, our portfolio is sought after the world over. Join us for a tasting in our attractive Tasting Room with a knowledgeable host in attendance and find out why!

  • Creation Estate Wines: R20 per wine
  • Creation Ridge Wines: R60 per wine
  • Creation Art of Chardonnay & Art of Pinot Noir: R120 per wine.

Individual wines are selected during your tasting experience.
