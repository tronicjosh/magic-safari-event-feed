Whale Watching and Marine Big 5 Tours in South Africa
Welcome to Marine Dynamics, an award-winning ecotourism and marine tourism company based in Gansbaai, South Africa. We are dedicated to providing exceptional whale watching, Marine Big 5, and shark cage diving experiences. As leaders in marine conservation, we live by the motto Discover | Protect | Educate; ensuring that every marine adventure not only thrills but also educates and fosters a deep appreciation for the marine environment. We are the top choice for whale watching and Marine Big 5 tours in South Africa and the only company with its own registered conservation trust.
Immerse yourself in the Dyer Island ecosystem and meet the Marine Big 5: whales, dolphins, seals, sharks, and the endangered African penguin. From January to May, you may encounter Humpback and Brydes whales, while from June to December, the majestic Southern Right Whales grace our waters. We are the only company in the industry where you can observe sharks from the boat year- round. A visit to Geyser Rock will show you thousands of Cape Fur seals and their neighbours, the endangered African penguins, along with various other seabirds breeding on Dyer Island. We also explore the bay to search for three species of playful dolphins.
Proudly Fair Trade certified since 2008 and boasting Blue Flag status for our vessels, we are your premier choice for responsible and exhilarating marine adventures.

Marine Dynamics Shark & Whale Tours species:
The Marine Big 5
One of the many ways in which Gansbaai is a truly unique, special prospect for people who want to work with marine wildlife, is how diverse the area is. Between The Marine Big 5 and countless other species, few places in the world are home to so many incredible animals.
Marine Big 5 Safaris near Cape Town
Species we work with:
Bronze Whaler Sharks
The Bronze whaler (also known as Copper sharks) here in Kleinbaai are very curious and interactive. These sharks eat mostly fish, with sardines being one of their favourites.
The Bronze Whaler Shark
Southern right whales
Southern right whales visit the bay between June to December. You can also see seasonal migrating Humpback whales and share the sea with our more resident Bryde’s whales.
The Southern Right Whale
Dolphins
Three species of dolphin visit our bay – the endangered Humpback dolphin, the common dolphin and the Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphin.
Dolphins
Cape fur seals
With a local population of 60,000 on Geyser Rock, our resident and very playful seals provide many photographic opportunities. Young pups are birthed around November and brave the waters around April.
The Cape fur seal
African penguins
One of 18 penguin species in the world, the endangered African penguin is endemic to South Africa and Namibia. See our feathered friends and their special penguin homes on Dyer Island.
The African penguin

About us
Marine Dynamics lives by the motto: Discover. Protect. Educate. We strive to provide you with the utmost conservation-oriented and respect ethos offered on the market. Our operations and boats are Fair Trade Tourism and Blue Flag certified.
We employ marine biologists to study this incredible ecosystem further. Using the daily observational platform, we have published numerous scientific papers and influenced conservation policies in South Africa.
We provide training and job opportunities for people from the local communities to become involved in the booming tourism industry. We pride ourselves in welcoming all walks of life and sharing our mission for the ocean to thrive for generations to come.
