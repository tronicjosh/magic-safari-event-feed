Celebrating Marine Magic: The Hermanus Whale Festival 2026

For 35 incredible years, Hermanus Whale Festival 2026 has celebrated the annual return of the majestic Southern Right Whales to the shores of Hermanus — creating one of South Africa’s most iconic coastal events.

Taking place from 2–4 October 2026, the Festival is proudly recognised as the world’s only Eco-Marine Festival, combining world-class land-based whale watching with ocean conservation, environmental education, local culture, live entertainment, and unforgettable family experiences.

Set in the heart of the breathtaking Cape Whale Coast during peak whale season, the Festival transforms Hermanus into a vibrant celebration of ocean life, creativity, community, and sustainable tourism.

Discover • Protect • Educate

At the heart of the Festival is a strong commitment to environmental responsibility, conservation awareness, sustainable tourism, and community upliftment.

The Hermanus Whale Festival brings together tourism, nature, education, arts, culture, wellness, and local business to showcase the very best of Hermanus and the Western Cape while supporting responsible eco-tourism and marine conservation.

Every year, the Festival attracts thousands of local and international visitors, creating valuable economic opportunities for accommodation establishments, restaurants, shops, tour operators, artists, crafters, and local entrepreneurs throughout the Overstrand region.

Whether you come for the whales, the atmosphere, the ocean experiences, or the vibrant coastal energy — the Hermanus Whale Festival offers an unforgettable celebration of nature, community, and the spirit of the Whale Coast.

About the Hermanus Whale Festival

The Hermanus Whale Festival is the world’s only Eco-Marine Festival, celebrating the arrival of the Southern Right Whales to the shores of Hermanus each year. Held in the heart of the Cape Whale Coast, the festival combines world-class land-based whale watching with ocean conservation, environmental education, live entertainment, local food, markets, family activities, marine exhibitions, and community-driven experiences.

Eco-Marine Village Highlights

The Eco-Marine Village is the educational heart of the Hermanus Whale Festival, featuring interactive marine exhibitions, ocean conservation displays, family-friendly activities, marine and nature talks, and hands-on experiences focused on the Marine Big 5 — whales, dolphins, penguins, seals, and sharks. Designed to inspire all ages, the village promotes environmental awareness through fun, learning, and discovery.
