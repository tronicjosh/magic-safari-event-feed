Sea Kayaking
in Walker Bay Whale Sanctuary

Hermanus Sea Kayaking in the Walker Bay Whale Sanctuary

Experience the best of Hermanus sea kayaking on an unforgettable adventure in the Walker Bay Whale Sanctuary. Paddle along the stunning coastline, home to rich marine life, dramatic sea cliffs, and awe-inspiring mountain views. Whether you’re an experienced kayaker or a first-timer, our stable, beginner-friendly kayaks make it easy for everyone to enjoy this one-of-a-kind experience.

Your Hermanus Sea Kayaking Adventure

Starting at the historic Old Harbour in Hermanus, your tour begins with a comprehensive kayaking safety and techniques briefing. Accompanied by a knowledgeable and passionate guide, you’ll explore the bay on a route tailored to the day’s sea conditions, wind, and your group’s abilities.

Marvel at the crystal-clear waters as your guide reveals the secrets of the ocean and its incredible wildlife. This is more than just a kayaking trip - it’s an intimate journey into the natural wonders of Walker Bay.

Why Choose Walker Bay Adventures for Hermanus Sea Kayaking?

We operate exclusively within a Marine Reserve, ensuring a safe and eco-conscious experience. During whale season, this area transforms into the Walker Bay Whale Sanctuary, a Marine Protected Area (MPA). While sightings of southern right whales are possible, our team adheres strictly to conservation laws, maintaining a respectful 300m distance.  If whales decide to come close to the kayaks we must move away from them.

Eco-Friendly and Sustainable Tourism

We prioritise the environment in all aspects of our operations. Our tours are designed to create minimal noise and no pollution. Additionally, we organise regular clean-ups to keep the bay free of litter, helping to preserve this natural treasure for future generations.

High-Quality Equipment and Expert Guides

Our Perception Tribe sea kayaks, imported from the UK, are designed for ultimate stability and comfort. These sit-on-top, self-bailing kayaks are ideal for all skill levels, so even first-timers can enjoy their time on the water.

Our professional guides are certified in marine guiding and first aid at sea. They are not only highly skilled but also deeply passionate about the ocean, ensuring your trip is as safe and educational as it is enjoyable.

Wildlife Encounters While Kayaking in Hermanus

The waters of Walker Bay are teeming with marine life, with the possibility of seeing:

  • Cape fur seals
  • African penguins (Now critically endangered)
  • Dolphins (Common, Bottlenose, and even Indo Pacific Humpback Dolphins)
  • Whales (Southern Right, Bryde’s and Humpback Whales at different times of year)
  • Cormorants and other seabirds

Every paddle offers the possibility to encounter these incredible creatures, making this an adventure to remember.

Tour Details
  • Duration: 2 hours (90 minutes of paddling, after the check in and thorough safety briefing)
  • No prior kayaking experience is required! 
  • What to bring:
    • Comfortable shorts and a T-shirt
    • Sunhat and sunscreen
    • A windbreaker or fleece (on cooler days)
    • A towel and a change of clothes for after the tour
    • Phone or Camera in waterproof housing (Optional). We also sell waterproof cell phone pouches at R200 each.

Note: Tours are subject to weather conditions. In the event of excessive wind or swell, we may reschedule or cancel trips to ensure your safety. Morning departures are recommended for calmer conditions, with specific times confirmed at booking.

Tour Times
  • November to April: 8:00, 10:30, and 13:00
  • May to October: 9:00, 11:30, and 14:00

Meeting & Departure Point

Our Hermanus sea kayaking tours start and end at the historic Hermanus Old Harbour, a central landmark rich in history and charm. Conveniently located in the heart of Hermanus, the Old Harbour is easily accessible and offers parking nearby. To find us, head to the Hermanus Waterfront area and follow the signs to the Old Harbour Museum. Our meeting point is just steps away from the picturesque shoreline, where your guide will be waiting to welcome you and provide all necessary instructions for the adventure ahead.
