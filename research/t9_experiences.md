## 4. What matters for a scraper

Booking engines (availability and often the price are in the engine, not on the page):
- Activitar: Marine Dynamics (marinedynamics.activitar.com), Dyer Island Cruises (dyer-island-cruises.activitar.com), African Horse Company (africanhorsecompany.activitar.com). A marketplace URL on activitar.com for a "Hermanus Whale-Watching Flight" (Ocean Guard Africa) redirected to network.activitar.com and gave no data.
- ActivityBridge (secure.activitybridge.com): Southern Right Charters (372), White Shark Projects (452), SA Forest zipline (332), quad biking (15317).
- Dineplan widget: Creation Wines table reservation.
- Q2B: Fly Aerovest. The page returned "Your venue is not active for this function".

Prices not in page text:
- PDF: Creation Wines seasonal pairing, à la carte and breakfast menus.
- On request: African Wings ("Personal quote", "Prices on request.").
- Not on the operator page, but on a reseller page: Southern Right Charters states no price; Walker Bay Adventures sells the same ActivityBridge activity (372) at "R1440 per adult, R720 per child under 12", "2 Hours", "June – November".
- Reseller price differs from operator price: shark cage diving through Walker Bay Adventures is "R1900 per person*" with "Discounted pricing applied on checkout" (ActivityBridge 452); White Shark Projects' own page says "R2,700 per international adult" and "R1,800 per South African adult".
- Two price tiers (international and South African) and a separate "R150 conservation fee": Marine Dynamics, Dyer Island Cruises, White Shark Projects (tiers only).
- Unlabelled numbers: the Marine Dynamics page has "ZAR 1 500.00" and "ZAR 900.00" with no label (possibly struck-through old prices).
- Possible image-only content: the Hermanus Whale Cruises home page gave no facts as text, and its booking element is an image. Not verified if facts are in images or on other pages.

Access problems:
- HTTP 403 for bouchardfinlayson.co.za (possible bot block).
- Masked or obfuscated contact data: Grootbos (phone and email behind "Show"), Hamilton Russell (email), African Horse Company (email).
- JavaScript: not tested. The tool does not run JavaScript, and I did not compare with a browser. All pages that loaded returned text.

Seasonal wording that differs for the same activity:
- Boat whale watching: Southern Right Charters "From June to December" and "June to end November" on one page; Walker Bay Adventures "June – November"; Dyer Island Cruises trips "January to December" with Southern Right whales June to December; Hermanus Whale Cruises states nothing.
- Whale flight: African Wings "Season · June to November"; Hermanus.co.za "is available during whale season" (no months); SA-Venues "Flights are weather dependent. Please enquire."
- Shark cage diving: Marine Dynamics "April through to August/September" but says the seasons are no longer distinct; the sister site says shark viewing is "year- round"; White Shark Projects states no season.
- Sea kayaking: departure times change by season (November to April, May to October), no closed period stated.
- Wine: Creation "Mon - Sun: 09h00 - 17h00 (Seasonal Hours Differ)", with no statement of what differs.

Status and location changes that a standing record must hold:
- Panthera Africa: "temporarily closed to visitors while we rebuild", no date.
- Zipline: the page title says Hermanus, the text says the tour moved to Caledon.
- Weather wording: "weather/sea permitting" (Southern Right Charters), "subject to weather conditions" (kayak), "sightings cannot be guaranteed" plus a voucher rule (Marine Dynamics), refund guarantee (African Wings on SA-Venues).

## 5. What I checked, what I did not check, odd results

Checked, and how:
- 5 web searches to find operators and lists.
- 30 page fetches: 17 operator or reseller pages that loaded, 8 list pages (Hermanus.co.za x2, SA-Venues x5, whalecoast.info x1), and 5 that failed (Creation guessed URL 404, Bouchard Finlayson 403, Ivanhoe guessed domain not found, Q2B "Access Denied", Activitar redirect).
- All 60 items of list B were read and classified.
- The counts in section 1 were made from the rows in section 2.

Not checked:
- Exact wording against raw HTML. All quotes came through the small model of the fetch tool.
- Bouchard Finlayson (403), Ivanhoe Sea Safaris, Fly Aerovest, Lady Stanford, Wine Hoppers: no operator page read.
- Deeper pages: Hamilton Russell (only the home page), Heart of Abalone /tours, Panthera /visits, Grootbos single experience pages, Hermanus Whale Cruises rates page, SA Forest quad and sandboarding pages, Marine Dynamics pages other than the cage diving page.
- The last 76,679 characters of the africanwings.co.za page (the tool read the first 100,000).
- Content inside booking engines, PDFs and images.
- Pages with JavaScript in a real browser.
- Drive times. No page that I read states a distance or drive time to Hermanus.
- Other candidate types that I did not search for: private boat charter, deep-sea fishing, scuba, paragliding, spa, private chef or restaurant experiences, Fernkloof guided walks, fat bike tours, golf.
- TripAdvisor, Viator, GetYourGuide lists.
- If each experience meets the luxury bar. That is the owner's decision.

Odd results:
- africanwings.co.za contains template placeholders ("[XX min]", "[AOC number]", "[base airfield]") and a form label "Safari lodge or airstrip". It gives no phone number. It may be an unfinished site, or not the same African Wings that SA-Venues lists with Hermanus phone numbers. Do not use it as a source until someone checks it.
- Hermanus.co.za link paths end with "index.html" under "component/k2", and 2 cards are "Advertise here". The page looks like an old static copy. Its scenic flights page names no operator. Freshness not checked.
- whalecoast.info/things-to-do is a news post, not a list.
- Walker Bay Adventures shows the kayak and shark combo at "R2250pp" in one banner and "R2200pp" in another.
- African Horse Company says "2-8 day" and "3 to 8 day" trails on the same page.
- Southern Right Charters gives two season ends on the same page (December, end November).
- The Marine Dynamics page states "we have divers as young as 4", but no minimum age rule and no age limit for the child price.
- The Dyer Island Cruises under-12 international price (R1725) is close to the adult local price (R1728). Read from the tool output; check on the page.
